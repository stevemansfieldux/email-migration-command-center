One platform, three ways to run it — what they save, what we keep

Everyone migrates onto the same product. The licence decides who runs it: they do (free licence), we do (flat managed fee), or it lives on our cloud (sliding licence, 12-month contract). The migration fee steps with the list: $4k under 75k, $5k, $6k, $8k over 250k. Pick a list size; edit the pricing at the bottom and everything recalculates. Our cost to serve is deliberately not modelled here — who does the work (us, agents, outsourced) is not decided.
